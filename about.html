Our house

Twenty-nine years of taking instructions from owners, not builders.

Atria is a private brokerage. We do not develop, we do not resell inventory, and we do not take a developer's marketing fee. Our only income is the fee our client agrees to.

The story

It started with a disputed carpet area.

In 2004 a client bought a Worli apartment sold as 4,200 sq ft. It measured 3,690. The developer pointed at a clause. We paid the difference out of our own fee and changed how we work: from that month, no residence goes on our books until one of us has measured it.

That is the whole idea. Every number on this site is one we took ourselves, and every drawing is one you can hold against the wall.
