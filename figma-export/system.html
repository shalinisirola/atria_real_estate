01

Foundations

Three rules hold the whole thing together.

Hairlines, not shadows

Structure comes from 1px rules at #E4E0D8. Elevation exists only for things that float above the page — modals, toasts, the search bar.

Gold is punctuation

The accent appears as plan marks, one primary action per view, and status. If two gold things compete in a frame, one is wrong.

Space is the luxury

Sections breathe at 128–160px. Nothing is allowed to fill a gap that was left deliberately.

  • Focus. A 2px gold-dim ring at 3px offset on every interactive element. Never removed, only restyled.
  • Keyboard. Tabs take arrow keys, modals trap focus and close on Escape, the skip link is the first thing in the tab order.
  • Labels. Every input has a visible label. Icon-only buttons carry aria-label; state lives in aria-pressed and aria-current.
  • Motion. prefers-reduced-motion cuts every transition, the hero draws instantly, and the ripple is skipped.
  • Text. Body copy never below 15px, line length capped at 66 characters, no text baked into a plate.
