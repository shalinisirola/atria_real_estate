Skyline Penthouse 4201

A full-floor penthouse on the forty-second storey, held by one family since handover in 2019 and never leased.

The plan turns its back on the road. Every principal room faces west across the Arabian Sea, and the living volume runs 19 metres without a single column. Ceilings are 3.7 m in the reception rooms and 3.1 m in the bedroom wing. Floors are honed Kota stone throughout, with oak in the two studies. The kitchen was rebuilt in 2023 by Studio Kilo in oiled teak and Corian.

The terrace is the reason to see it in person: 1,400 sq ft, planted with frangipani and curry leaf, with the pool set to catch the last forty minutes of light.
